About the Artist

Jagan S

Biotechnology graduate. Healthcare strategist. Artist.

I studied biotechnology and practice art. They're closer than they sound.

In Bangalore, I filled margins with doodles, faces, shapes, lines that came without much thought. Instinct. Even through a biotechnology degree, art stayed with me between lectures and lab work.

Now I work in healthcare marketing strategy and paint, draw, create not as a hobby, as parallel practice. Graphite, charcoal, oil, acrylic, watercolor, pastel, mixed media. I'm exploring sculpture and modelling; each material teaches something the others don't, and I follow the idea rather than forcing the medium.

Realism is where I start portraits, birds, animals, the natural world but I leave space for exploration. Growth happens outside comfort, and I try to visit often.

My work has been shown at exhibitions. Commissions completed for international clients concept through delivery, signed originals with certificates.

Discipline and creativity aren't opposites. They're partners. One shows up. The other surprises. Together they make the work possible.
