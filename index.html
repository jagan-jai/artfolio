About

The Artist

I make things across a few different mediums — oil on canvas, digital prints, photographs, and mixed media work. The medium changes, but the practice is the same: looking closely, making slowly, and leaving room for accident.

This artfolio is the canonical collection — each work here has a place, a date, and a note about how it came to be. It's not a feed; it's a record. For daily process and works in progress, I post on Instagram.

I work alone, in a small studio, and I show my work when it feels ready — not on a schedule. This site is updated as the work accumulates.
